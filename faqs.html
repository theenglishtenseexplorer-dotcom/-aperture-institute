Getting to know APERTURE

What is APERTURE Institute?

APERTURE Institute is a modern online learning academy offering programs in English language development, examination preparation, professional communication, and educator development.

Is APERTURE a physical school?

No. APERTURE is an online learning academy. All learning arrangements are designed for online delivery.

Who can study at APERTURE?

Our programs are designed for both local and international learners. Individual course requirements may vary.

Programs

What courses are available?

We offer four academic divisions: APERTURE English, Examination Preparation, APERTURE Professional, and Educator Development.

Do you offer IELTS and TOEFL preparation?

Yes. Both are included in our Examination Preparation division.

Do you offer courses for teachers?

Yes. Our Educator Development division focuses on teaching methodology, language instruction, and professional growth.

Learning arrangements

Are classes conducted online?

Yes. Our learning arrangements are online.

Can I choose between live classes and self-paced courses?

Both options are part of our learning model. Availability depends on the course.

Can I study from another country?

Yes. Our online learning model is intended to serve learners locally and internationally.

Enrollment

How do I enroll?

Submit an inquiry through our Contact page. We will provide information about the relevant program and enrollment process.

How much do courses cost?

Fees depend on the specific program and learning arrangement. Details will be provided during the inquiry process.

Do I need to create an account to access the website?

No. Our public website does not require registration or login.

Have another question in mind?

Send us a message and we will be glad to help you explore your options.
